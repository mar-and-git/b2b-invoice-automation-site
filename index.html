Local Windows application · Private use

Financial document intake and control, with human oversight.

B2B Invoice Automation helps the application owner collect, review, and reconcile business financial documents. It runs locally on Windows and integrates with selected document sources.

Not a public SaaS service

Document sources

Uses financial records from the owner's configured sources, including an invoicing system and approved document storage.

Evidence matching

Connects invoice details with supporting documents, such as fuel transaction receipts, and highlights incomplete or conflicting evidence for review.

Controlled operations

Document review, approval and financial processing remain subject to separate permissions and explicit workflow controls.

Why the application requests Gmail access

The owner may connect a designated Gmail account to find and read messages containing supporting financial documents, such as detailed BP fuel invoices and prepaid settlement notes. Gmail data is used to identify the relevant attachments, verify document identity and match transaction evidence with existing invoice records.

Gmail permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. Google grants this permission across the connected mailbox; the application is designed to restrict its normal retrieval to matching business-document messages. It does not require permission to send, delete, or modify email.

Gmail checks run when the owner explicitly refreshes sources in the Operator. Downloaded evidence does not automatically become a second accounting invoice, and email access does not itself authorize financial processing.

How user data is handled

OAuth credentials are held in protected local Windows storage, and downloaded files are processed using controlled document-handling components. The application may keep supporting records needed for review and audit. Read our privacy policy for details about access, storage, permissions, revocation and retention.
